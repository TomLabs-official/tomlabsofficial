02 / Principles

Clarity beats complexity.

We prefer products that explain themselves. The interface should do most of the talking. Documentation should fill in the gaps—not rescue confusing design.

01 — USEFUL

Useful over impressive

A feature earns its place by solving a real problem.

02 — CRAFT

Details matter

Typography, spacing, loading states, errors, and small interactions are part of the product.

03 — SHIP

Build, release, learn

Real users teach you things a perfect planning document never will.
